Contact

Get in touch

This site has no booking system — the quickest way to reach us is WhatsApp. Tap the button below and it opens a message ready to send, or message us directly with your own words.

Book your stay

Tap the button below to open WhatsApp with a message ready to send. Include whatever you can of the following, so we can reply with real availability and pricing:

  • Your name
  • Arrival date and number of nights
  • Number of guests
  • What you're interested in — game drives, bush walks, cultural experiences, and so on
  • Anything else useful — dietary needs, mobility considerations, questions

Book now on WhatsApp

This opens WhatsApp with a starting message you can edit before sending — nothing is submitted or stored by this website. Nothing here is a booking confirmation; we'll reply to confirm availability.

If you'd rather message directly

Not filling in the form is completely fine — a message with your name, dates, number of guests and what you're interested in is all we need to get started.

Rates

From ~$12.50 per person a night for cyclists and ~$37.50 for general tourists, tent hire at ~$25, and guided game drives at around ~$30 per person (figures are approximate USD, converted from KSh). See the full breakdown on the Stay page, and send us your dates and group size for an exact total.

We're often out on game drives or hosting guests, and out of mobile signal for stretches of the day. We reply as soon as we're back at camp — usually the same day, sometimes the next.

Finding us

Directions

Naisuyo Campsite is in Laikipia County, Kenya, on the Ewaso Nyiro River.

Once your stay is confirmed, we'll send you a location pin and driving directions directly on WhatsApp — the last stretch of road in particular is easier to describe than to write down.

While you're here

Photographing people

Please ask before photographing any member of our community, and respect it if someone would rather not be photographed. Photos of the landscape and wildlife are welcome, but a person's image is theirs to give, not something to take because they happen to be in frame.
