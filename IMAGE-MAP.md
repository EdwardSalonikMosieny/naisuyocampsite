## What's missing — worth shooting today or tomorrow

1. **A wide establishing shot of the whole camp** — tents, manyatta structures and the landscape together in one frame. Right now home/stay have to lean on a single tent photo and a single hut photo; nothing shows the camp as a place.
2. **Tent interiors** — bedding, mosquito netting, a made-up bed. There is currently exactly one accommodation photo (the exterior tent shot) for the entire Stay page.
3. **Bathroom/shower/toilet facilities** — whatever exists (long-drop, bucket shower, whatever). Guests booking a campsite want to know this before they arrive.
4. **Food** — a plated meal, the cooking area, anything. There's a full "what to bring / what to expect" page with zero food photography.
5. **A campfire at night** — the Relaxation section (campfire, local brew, storytelling) is a named, distinct part of the offering and currently has no supporting photo at all.
6. **The Ewaso Nyiro River itself** — it's in the very first sentence of the positioning statement and doesn't appear in a single photo.
7. **Lion, cheetah, or giraffe** — all three are named explicitly in the copy ("elephant, lion, reticulated giraffe and cheetah") but none appear in the photo set. Whatever you actually have shots of should replace or supplement the copy — I have not invented sightings you can't back up.
8. **A single consented portrait of a guide or host**, ideally looking at camera, for the "our guides are experts and conservationists" framing on About/Experiences — the closest thing right now is a ceremony crowd shot.
9. **Daytime wide landscape of the Laikipia scrub/hills** without people or animals, for use as secondary hero/background material.

If any of the 8 excluded tourist photos are actually fine to use (i.e., you have consent, or they're stock/marketing photos your guests knowingly posed for), tell me and I'll bring the ones you approve back in — cropped tighter where possible to de-emphasise identifiable faces.
