Camping inside a working manyatta

Naisuyo is a tented campsite set inside our own manyatta, so your stay is bordered by the same structures, fires and daily routines our community lives with — not a separate compound built to look at it from a distance.

What to expect

The campsite is simple and well looked after — ask us on WhatsApp for anything not covered here before you travel.

  • Bonfire and lighting

    An evening bonfire and night lighting around camp, so moving between your tent and the common areas after dark is easy.

  • Security

    Camp security is in place overnight.

  • Tables, chairs and restrooms

    Tables and chairs for meals and downtime, and basic restroom facilities on site.

  • Meals

    Breakfast and dinner are available on request — let us know when you enquire.

  • Power and water

    Mobile signal in the area comes and goes with the terrain. Confirm power, charging and drinking-water arrangements with us directly before you travel.

What it costs

Prices are per person per night unless stated otherwise, shown in USD. Figures are converted at roughly KSh 160 to the dollar, so treat them as an approximate guide — your bank or M-Pesa rate may differ slightly, and we're happy to confirm the exact total in either currency when you enquire.

Packing for camp

A general packing list for a Laikipia tented camp — check with us for anything specific to your dates.

  • Warm layers for cold early mornings and evenings, even though days are hot
  • A torch or headlamp for moving around camp after dark
  • Sun protection — hat, sunscreen, sunglasses
  • Comfortable closed shoes for bush walks
  • A reusable water bottle
  • Cash for any incidentals, since card payment is unlikely to be available this far from town
